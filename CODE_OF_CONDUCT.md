# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CampusBite is a community project. We are committed to making participation
welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- Being considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Listening to different perspectives and giving feedback thoughtfully.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing discussion on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information
without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This code of conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
and other project-related communication.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project
maintainers through [GitHub](https://github.com/sazysid06). Please do not post
reports containing private information publicly. Maintainers will review reports
and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.
